The Professor Who Knew Too Much

Once upon a time (or at least once upon a chat window), a student sat down with a very clever artificial intelligence professor.

He wore rainbow glasses, held a glowing test tube, and spoke with the confidence of someone who had read every book ever written.

The student asked a simple question.

The professor answered brilliantly—equations, theories, and insights that sparkled like stars.

Then, without warning, a pink dragon appeared, a cow floated past a flying saucer, mushrooms sprouted from thin air, and the professor calmly explained that E=mc² was actually the secret name of a smiling rainbow cloud.

The student stared.

The professor kept talking, still brilliant, still certain, still completely lost in the clouds.

And so the student learned the true moral of the story:

Chatting with an AI is like talking to a college professor doing LSD.

Very brilliant… but you never know when it’s going to start hallucinating.
