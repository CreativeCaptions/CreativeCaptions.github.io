Safeguard American Voter Eligibility and Access (SAVE America) Act

A Proposal for Secure Federal Elections with Verified Eligibility

Overview

The purpose of the Safeguard American Voter Eligibility and Access (SAVE America) Act is to establish strong safeguards ensuring that only United States citizens register and vote in federal elections.

This framework requires documented proof of citizenship for federal voter registration and secure identification requirements for federal elections while ensuring that eligible citizens have access to the necessary documentation before participating.

Section 1: Purpose

The United States shall maintain secure federal elections by requiring verification of citizenship and identity before registration and voting in federal elections.

The purpose of this Act is to establish consistent standards that protect the integrity of federal elections.

Section 2: Proof of Citizenship for Federal Voter Registration

Requirement

No state or local election official shall accept or process an application to register to vote in a federal election unless the applicant provides documentary proof of United States citizenship.

Acceptable Documentation

Acceptable proof of citizenship shall include:

  • A REAL ID-compliant driver's license or state-issued identification card indicating U.S. citizenship.
  • A certified birth certificate accompanied by government-issued photo identification.
  • A naturalization certificate or certificate of citizenship.
  • A military identification card accompanied by acceptable evidence of U.S. citizenship.

Documentation Access

To ensure eligible citizens have the opportunity to comply with these requirements:

  • Federal and state agencies, including vital records offices, departments of motor vehicles, and other relevant agencies, shall provide required voting-related documents at no cost to eligible citizens who certify the documents are needed for voter registration purposes.
  • States shall provide accessible procedures for obtaining required documentation, including:
    • Online and mail applications.
    • Free delivery or pickup options.
    • Expedited processing where practical.
    • Extended hours where practical.
    • Assistance for elderly citizens, individuals with disabilities, rural residents, and citizens facing hardship.
  • The federal government may provide grants to assist states with implementation costs.

Section 3: Photo Identification Requirements for Federal Elections

Identity Verification Requirement

Voters participating in federal elections shall present government-issued photo identification before casting a ballot.

Acceptable identification shall include:

  • A REAL ID-compliant driver's license.
  • A REAL ID-compliant state identification card.
  • Other government-issued photo identification meeting federal standards.

Identification requirements shall be completed and verified before a ballot is counted.

Section 4: Mail-In and Absentee Ballot Security Requirements

Secure Return Deadline

States shall establish a clear return deadline for mail-in and absentee ballots in federal elections that provides election officials sufficient time for verification, processing, and reporting.

Secure Ballot Return Options

States shall provide secure ballot return options, including official election drop boxes or other authorized return locations available through Election Day.

Mail Ballot Identification Verification

States shall verify the identity of mail-in and absentee voters through secure methods connected to voter registration records and existing government systems.

Verification may include:

  • Matching voter registration information.
  • Government-issued identification records.
  • Other approved security procedures established under federal standards.

Identity verification shall be completed before a mail-in or absentee ballot is counted.

Section 5: Verification, Security, and Implementation

Voter Roll Maintenance

States shall maintain accurate voter registration records and shall use available federal and state databases, including citizenship verification systems, Social Security Administration records, and other lawful sources, for verification purposes.

Penalties

Existing penalties for knowingly submitting false claims of citizenship or committing election fraud shall remain in effect.

Public Education

Federal and state governments shall provide public information explaining:

  • Citizenship documentation requirements.
  • Identification requirements.
  • Registration procedures.
  • Available methods for obtaining required documents before elections.

Implementation Timeline

Implementation shall occur through a reasonable transition period allowing time for:

  • System development.
  • Staff training.
  • Public notification.
  • Document access programs.

Enforcement

The Attorney General may bring civil actions to enforce compliance with this Act.

The Election Assistance Commission may monitor implementation and issue guidance to assist states in meeting requirements.

Additional Provisions

  • States shall comply with all requirements established under this Act for federal elections.
  • Reasonable accommodations shall remain available for active-duty military members, overseas voters, and individuals with disabilities.

Conclusion

The Safeguard American Voter Eligibility and Access (SAVE America) Act establishes a clear standard for federal elections by requiring verified citizenship and identity before registration and voting.

The framework ensures that election security requirements are paired with government responsibility to make necessary documentation obtainable before Election Day.

Secure elections require both accurate voter eligibility standards and consistent enforcement of those standards.
